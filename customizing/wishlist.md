# Wishlist
{: .no_toc }

{% include where.html path="Theme settings → Wishlist" styles="Drawer: Affluence, Bloom · Modal: Noir, Terra · Page: Atelier · Off: Catalog" %}

Shoppers save products with the heart button on product cards and product pages, then open their list from the heart icon in the header.

![The wishlist drawer open with saved products and Move to cart buttons]({{ '/assets/images/wishlist/drawer.png' | relative_url }})

![The wishlist drawer on a phone, sliding up from the bottom]({{ '/assets/images/wishlist/drawer-mobile.png' | relative_url }}){: .phone}
*On a phone.*

## How it works

The wishlist is stored in the shopper's own browser. There is no account, no app and no customer data sent anywhere. This is why it works with no setup but also means a wishlist does not follow a shopper to another device or browser.

## Setting it up

1. Check that **Theme settings → Wishlist → Enable wishlist** is on. Turning it off removes every wishlist button and the header icon.
1. In **Theme settings → Wishlist**, choose **Wishlist type**: *Drawer*, *Modal* or *Page*.
1. For *Page* only: create a page (**Online Store → Pages → Add page**) with the **Wishlist** template, then set **Wishlist page** in the **Header** section to that page. The header heart icon appears once this is set. *Drawer* and *Modal* need no page.
1. Optional: show a "Move to cart" button, confirm before clearing, and a notification when an item is removed.
1. Show or hide the heart on product cards in **Product cards**, and choose its position.

Shoppers can also move items from the cart to the wishlist: see [Move to wishlist](../cart/move-to-wishlist/). See the [Wishlist page section](../../reference/sections/main-wishlist/) for layout settings.

# Stock and other blocks

{% include where.html path="Product template → Product information → Add block" %}

## Inventory status

Shows "Only 3 left" when stock is low. It uses your real inventory and only appears for products that track inventory. Set **Low stock threshold** to the quantity at which it appears.

{: .note }
Lush never shows made-up stock levels, viewer counts or timers. Every number comes from your store.

## Pickup availability

Shows whether the selected variant can be picked up in store, with a link to every pickup location that stocks it, including ones where it's sold out. With one location, the link shows that store's address. Set up local pickup in **Settings → Shipping and delivery** first.

## Complementary products

"Pairs well with" products, shown under the buy buttons. Choose them per product in the **Shopify Search & Discovery** app, under **Product recommendations**. The block hides itself on products with none.

## Quick order trigger

For products with many variants, such as sizes or pack quantities. Adds a button that opens a table where shoppers enter quantities for every variant and add them all at once. It only shows on products with more than one variant.

## Icon with text

Up to three short perks with icons, such as "Free shipping over $150", "30-day returns" and "Secure checkout". Choose **Horizontal** or **Vertical**.

## Share and Wishlist

**Share** adds share links and a copy-link button. **Wishlist** adds a save-for-later heart. See [Wishlist](../../wishlist/).

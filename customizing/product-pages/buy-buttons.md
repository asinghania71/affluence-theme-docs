# Buy buttons

## Buy buttons block

Select the **Buy buttons** block in **Product information**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show quantity selector** | Lets shoppers choose a quantity before adding to cart |
| **Show accelerated checkout buttons** | Shows Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay and other one-tap payment buttons available to the shopper |

Quantity rules you set for B2B customers (minimum, maximum and increments) are applied to the quantity selector automatically.

## Sticky add-to-cart bar

A slim bar with the product name, price and an add-to-cart button that appears once shoppers scroll past the main button.

1. Select the **Product information** section (not a block).
2. Turn on **Show sticky add-to-cart bar**.
3. Choose **Position**: **Bottom** or **Top**.
4. Click **Save**.

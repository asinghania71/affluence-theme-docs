# Building the home page
{: .no_toc }

{% include where.html path="Page picker → Home page" %}

1. TOC
{:toc}

Open the theme editor and choose **Home page** in the page picker. Every block of the page is a section you can add, remove, hide or reorder.

## A proven order

1. **Hero** or **Slideshow**: one clear message and button.
2. **Collection list**: send shoppers to your main categories.
3. **Featured products**: a best-selling collection.
4. **Image with text**: your brand story or a key benefit.
5. **Trust badges**: free returns, secure checkout, shipping info.
6. **Testimonials**: real customer words.
7. **Email signup banner**.

## Choosing a banner section

| Section | Choose it when |
|---|---|
| [Hero](../../reference/sections/hero/) | One strong image or video with text beside or over it |
| [Slideshow](../../reference/sections/slideshow/) | Several campaigns that rotate, with arrows, dots or a progress bar |
| [Video](../../reference/sections/video/) | A brand film. Supports uploaded video and YouTube or Vimeo |
| [Collage](../../reference/sections/collage/) / [Promo grid](../../reference/sections/promo-grid/) | A magazine-style mix of tiles |

## Showing products

| Section | Choose it when |
|---|---|
| [Featured products](../../reference/sections/featured-collection/) | One collection as a grid or carousel |
| [Multi-collection tabs](../../reference/sections/multi-collection-tabs/) | Several collections in tabs, saving vertical space |
| [Featured product](../../reference/sections/featured-product/) | A single hero product with full buy controls |
| [Shop the look](../../reference/sections/shop-the-look/) | Tappable hotspots on a styled photo |
| [Shoppable shorts](../../reference/sections/shoppable-shorts/) | Short vertical videos with product tags |
| [Comparison table](../../reference/sections/comparison-table/) | Side-by-side products or plans |
| [Recently viewed](../../reference/sections/recently-viewed/) | Returning shoppers see what they looked at |

## Tips

- Keep the first screen focused: one message, one button.
- Always add **alt text** to images (in the admin file or media details) so screen readers can describe them.
- Use the **Mobile image** option on banners where a different crop works better on phones.
- Turn on **Reveal sections on scroll** in Animations for a polished feel. It switches off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

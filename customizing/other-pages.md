# Blog, pages and accounts
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Blog

The [Blog posts](../../reference/sections/main-blog/) section controls date, author, excerpt, columns and articles per page. A single post uses [Blog post](../../reference/sections/main-article/).

## Pages

Use **Online Store → Pages** and assign a template: **Default**, **About**, **Contact** or **Wishlist**. The **Contact form** section lets you add your own fields.

## Customer accounts

Login, register, account, order, addresses, activate-account and reset-password pages are included and styled to match your theme. Enable and configure accounts in **Settings → Customer accounts**.

## Password page

While your store is password protected, visitors see the [Password](../../reference/sections/password/) page. Set the message and colors in the editor.

## 404 page

The [404 page](../../reference/sections/main-404/) offers a way back to your shop.

# Transparent header

Lay the header over a full-width image at the top of the page, for a magazine-style first impression. The header turns solid as soon as shoppers scroll.

![A transparent header with a white logo over a full-width hero image]({{ '/assets/images/header/transparent.png' | relative_url }})

![The transparent header over the slideshow on a phone]({{ '/assets/images/header/transparent-mobile.png' | relative_url }}){: .phone}
*On a phone.*

1. Open the **Header** section.
2. Under **Transparent header**, choose **On the home page only** or **On every page**.
3. Choose a **Transparent color scheme**. Pick a scheme with light text for dark images, or dark text for light images.
4. Optional: upload a **Logo while transparent**, for example a white version of your logo.
5. Click **Save**.

The header only becomes transparent on pages whose first section is a **Hero**, a **Slideshow** or a full-width **Video** with no top padding. On every other page it stays solid, so text never ends up over the wrong background.

{: .note }
Adding a **Header menu row** makes the header solid, because a second row over an image is hard to read.

# Search
{: .no_toc }

{% include where.html path="Theme settings → Search" styles="Recent searches on: Affluence, Catalog" %}

## Predictive search

As shoppers type, matching products and suggestions appear. Suggestions can be navigated with the keyboard and are announced to screen readers.

![The search drawer showing product suggestions]({{ '/assets/images/search/predictive.png' | relative_url }})

![Search on a phone opens full screen, with page and product suggestions]({{ '/assets/images/search/predictive-mobile.png' | relative_url }}){: .phone}
*On a phone.*

In **Theme settings → Search**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show suggestions as customers type** | When off, pressing Enter goes straight to the results page |
| **Show product vendor**, **Show product price** | Details shown with each suggestion |
| **Suggested searches** | A menu of links shown before shoppers type, such as popular collections. Create it in **Content → Menus** |
| **Show recent searches** | Lists the shopper's last searches. Saved in their browser only |

To remove search from the header completely, turn off **Show search** in the **Header** section. The search page stays available.

## Results page

The [Search results](../../reference/sections/main-search/) section controls the heading, columns and a **No results** state: a popular-searches menu and suggested products, so shoppers never land on a dead end.

Filters and sorting on the results page come from the **Search & Discovery** app, the same as collection pages.

## Improving results

Search quality depends on your product data: clear titles, descriptions, tags, and product types. Use Search & Discovery to add synonyms and boost products.

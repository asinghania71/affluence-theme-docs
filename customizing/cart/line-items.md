# Line items and the cart page

## Choose what each item shows

1. In the page picker, choose **Cart**, then open the **Cart** section.
2. Select the **Cart items** block.
3. Turn details on or off: **Show vendor**, **Show title**, **Show variant options**, **Show price**, **Show quantity selector**, **Show line discounts**, **Show unit price**, **Show low stock badge**, **Show remove button**.
4. Click **Save**.

**Image width** (in the section settings) sets how wide the product photo is, as a share of the item row.

## Let shoppers choose which items to check out

**Let customers choose which items to check out** (on by default) adds a checkbox to each item. Unticked items stay in the cart for later. **Show selected items subtotal** shows a count of the ticked items.

## The cart page

The cart page uses the same section. On a computer, items sit on the left and the order summary stays in view on the right as shoppers scroll. On a phone, the checkout button stays pinned to the bottom of the screen.

Under **Cart page** in the section settings you can set the **Heading size** and **Top and bottom padding**. Sections you add to the Cart template appear below the cart.

The section also accepts content blocks, such as **Rich text**, **Hero banner**, **Trust badges**, **Payment methods**, **Featured collection**, **Carousel** and **Order note**.

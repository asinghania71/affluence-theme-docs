# Move to wishlist

Add a **Move to wishlist** link to each cart item. It saves the product to the shopper's wishlist and takes it out of the cart, so a shopper who isn't ready to buy can keep the item instead of deleting it.

1. Check that the wishlist is on: **Theme settings → Wishlist → Enable wishlist**.
2. Open **Theme settings → Cart**.
3. Turn on **Show "Move to wishlist" on cart items**.
4. Click **Save**.

![A cart item with Remove and Move to wishlist links under the quantity]({{ '/assets/images/cart/move-to-wishlist.png' | relative_url }})

{: .note }
The wishlist is saved in the shopper's browser. See [Wishlist](../../wishlist/) for how it works.

# Cart type

{% include where.html path="Theme settings → Cart → Cart type" styles="Drawer: Affluence, Noir, Terra · Page: Atelier · Notification: Bloom, Catalog" %}

Choose what shoppers see right after they add a product to the cart.

1. In the theme editor, open **Theme settings** (paintbrush icon).
2. Open **Cart**.
3. Under **Cart type**, choose an option.
4. Click **Save**.

| Option | What shoppers see | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawer** | A panel slides in with the full cart and a checkout button | Most stores. Shoppers keep browsing without leaving the page |
| **Page** | They go straight to the cart page | Stores whose shoppers usually buy one item and check out |
| **Notification** | A small "Added to your bag" confirmation with a **View bag** button | Large catalogs where shoppers add many items in a row |

![The "Added to your bag" notification in the top corner of a product page, with a View bag button]({{ '/assets/images/cart/notification.png' | relative_url }})
*Cart type set to Notification.*

![The add-to-cart notification on a phone]({{ '/assets/images/cart/notification-mobile.png' | relative_url }}){: .phone}
*On a phone.*

{: .note }
Each [style](../../../getting-started/styles/) picks a cart type for you. Changing it here doesn't affect anything else in the style.

To change which side the drawer slides in from, go to **Theme settings → Layout → Drawers** and set **Opens from (desktop)** and **Opens from (mobile)**. This also applies to the quick view and wishlist drawers.

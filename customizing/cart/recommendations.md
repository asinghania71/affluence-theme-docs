# Cart recommendations

Show "You may also like" products in the cart to raise the order value.

1. In the page picker, choose **Cart**, then open the **Cart** section.
2. Add a **Recommendations** block if there isn't one already.
3. In the section settings, under **Recommendations**, choose a **Source**:
   - **Automatic**: complementary products from the Shopify Search & Discovery app, based on the first item in the cart.
   - **Specific collection**: always shows products from the **Collection** you pick.
4. Set **Heading** and **Products to show**.
5. Click **Save**.

![A row of recommended products under the cart items]({{ '/assets/images/cart/recommendations.png' | relative_url }})

{: .note }
Pick a **Collection** even with automatic recommendations. It fills the space when the cart is empty or when no complementary products exist.

**Prioritize free-shipping unlocks** (on by default) puts a product first that would push the cart over the [free shipping threshold](../free-shipping-bar/).

# Image sizes

Shopify resizes your images automatically for each screen, so you only upload one version of each. Lush serves images up to **2000 pixels wide**. Larger uploads look no sharper; they just take longer to upload.

| Where | Recommended size | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Hero, slideshow, full-width banners | 2000 × 1000 px | Landscape. Add a separate **Mobile image** where offered, around 1000 × 1250 px |
| Image with text, collage tiles | 1500 × 1500 px | Square or portrait |
| Product images | 2000 × 2000 px (square) or 1600 × 2000 px (portrait) | Use the same shape for every product |
| Collection images | 1500 × 1500 px | Square |
| Logo | 500 px wide, PNG or SVG with a transparent background | Any |
| Favicon | 32 × 32 px or larger | Square |
| Social sharing image | 1200 × 628 px | Landscape |
| Blog post images | 1600 × 900 px | Landscape |

## Tips

- **Keep product images the same shape.** Mixed shapes make the grid uneven. If they differ, set **Theme settings → Product cards → Image ratio** to **Portrait** or **Square** to crop them evenly.
- **Set a focal point.** After uploading, open the image in **Content → Files** (or on the product) and click **Edit → Focal point**. With **Use image focal points** on (**Theme settings → Media**), the subject stays in view when an image is cropped.
- **Write alt text** for every image, describing what it shows. Screen readers read it aloud, and search engines use it.
- **Use JPG for photos** and PNG or SVG for logos and graphics. Keep files under 2 MB.

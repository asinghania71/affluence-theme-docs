# Updating the theme

New versions of Lush add features and fix bugs. See what's new in the [Changelog](../../changelog/).

## When an update is available

Shopify shows a notice on the theme in **Online Store → Themes**.

1. Click **Update** (or **View update**) on the theme card. Shopify adds the new version to your theme library as a copy, and keeps your current theme untouched.
2. Your settings, sections and content from the theme editor are carried over to the copy.
3. Click **Customize** on the new copy and check your key pages: home, a product, a collection and the cart.
4. When everything looks right, click **Publish**.

{: .warning }
**Code edits are not carried over.** If you or a developer changed the theme's code, those changes must be made again on the new version. Keep a note of every code change you make.

## Rolling back

Your previous version stays in the theme library. To roll back, click **⋯ → Publish** on it.

## What's in Lush

- **Six styles**: Lush, Noir, Atelier, Bloom, Terra and Catalog. Each is a full set of colors, fonts, corner shapes, spacing and page layouts.
- **Cart your way**: slide-out drawer, cart page or a small notification, with an optional free-shipping progress bar.
- **Wishlist without an app**: saved in the shopper's browser, no account needed.
- **Fast, accessible storefront**: works without JavaScript for core shopping tasks, respects reduced-motion settings, and is built for keyboard and screen-reader use.
- **31 languages** included.

# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- **Six styles**: Lush, Noir, Atelier, Bloom, Terra and Catalog.
- **65 sections**, each with its own color scheme and spacing.
- **Header**: four layouts, transparent header, hide-on-scroll and shrink-on-scroll, mega menu promos, an optional menu row and collection row, and an announcement bar with a countdown to a fixed date.
- **Product page**: five gallery layouts, adjustable gallery width, fit-to-screen media, variant images, swatches, custom fields, estimated delivery, tabs, specifications, size-guide pop-ups, quick order table and a sticky add-to-cart bar.
- **Cart**: drawer, page or notification; free shipping bar; remove with confirmation or undo; move to wishlist; terms checkbox; discount codes; gift wrap with message; shipping estimate; recommendations.
- **Wishlist** saved in the shopper's browser, as a drawer, pop-up or page. No app or account needed.
- **Collections and search**: filters, sorting, grid density toggle, product ribbons, badges, predictive search with suggested and recent searches.
- **31 languages** included.

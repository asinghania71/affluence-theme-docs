## Before you contact us

1. Search this documentation. The [FAQ](../faq/) page covers the most common questions.
2. Check the [Reference](../reference/) for the setting you are working with.
3. If something looks broken, try the theme on a duplicate with all apps disabled to rule out an app conflict.

## Contact support

**[Open the support form](https://forms.gle/YGrhru8Q67tTB3wa8)**{: .btn .btn-primary }

The form asks for:

- Your name (first and last)
- Your email address
- Your store URL, for example `your-store.myshopify.com`
- A description of the problem, plus an optional link to screenshots or a screen recording

You receive an automatic confirmation email when your request is sent.

## Support policy

*Edit the commitments below to match what you can really deliver before publishing.*

- **What is covered:** bugs in the theme, questions about how to use its features and settings, and help getting the theme to behave as documented.
- **What is not covered:** custom code changes, third-party app issues, store content, and design or development work beyond the theme's own settings. A Shopify Partner can help with those.
- **Response time:** we aim to reply within TWO business days (Monday to Friday).
- **Bug fixes:** confirmed bugs are fixed in a theme update, which you receive through your theme library.

## Editing theme code

{: .warning }
Customizations made directly in the code can be overwritten when you update the theme, and mistakes can break your storefront. **Duplicate your theme before editing any code**, and consider hiring a [Shopify Partner](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory) for custom development.
